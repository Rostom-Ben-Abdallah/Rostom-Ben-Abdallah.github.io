01 / RESEARCH

From multi-camera video to behaviour understanding.

My 2026 Mitacs research addressed the challenge of maintaining reliable identity and behaviour evidence across cameras despite occlusion, crowded scenes and changes in appearance.

Mitacs Globalink · 2026Computer visionTemporal modelling

AI-Based Computer Vision for Vervet Monkey Identification & Spatiotemporal Behaviour Recognition

I designed an end-to-end platform for vervet-monkey monitoring with body detection, collar-based individual identification, multi-camera evidence, dataset validation and video behaviour recognition. My original contribution includes Vervet-TCM, a custom temporal architecture evaluated alongside a 3D ResNet-18 video baseline.

ORIGINAL MODEL DESIGN

Vervet-TCM · Custom temporal behaviour model

A purpose-built model combining appearance and motion evidence, temporal convolutions and learned attention for causal video-based behaviour classification. Benchmarked against R3D-18 with a complementary fusion experiment.
